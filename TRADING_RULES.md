# Owner trading rules and bounded research implementation

Recorded September 18, 2026 from the owner's revised 32-profile request.

- Every position must close **before 4:00 PM Chicago time**. No position may
  survive a daily market closure or the weekend closure.
- Holding across midnight within an open overnight session is permitted. A fresh
  Sunday evening entry after the legal reopening is not a weekend-held position.
- The intended morning entry window is **7:00 AM inclusive to 10:30 AM exclusive,
  America/Chicago**. It restricts new executions, not protective management.
- The research uses a proposed **3:55 PM Chicago** operational deadline, five
  minutes before the owner's boundary. An earlier scheduled market close takes
  priority with the same five-minute lead. This buffer is not an additional owner
  rule or a statement of every prop firm's rules.
- Entries are locked at the deadline until actual legal reopening. Pending entry
  orders are canceled; necessary protection remains effective until closure is
  confirmed. Analytical setups and market history are preserved.
- Historical research closes a remaining position at the recorded complete
  one-minute close ending at the deadline, after stop-first protective handling.
  Missing boundary prices and unresolved or overdue positions fail explicitly.
  Partial gains/losses and the original 0.514-point round-trip costs count once.
- Legacy unrestricted holding and the old 6:00 AM–9:30 AM Chicago morning preset
  remain available solely with their historical identities. Neither is a member
  of the new daily-close comparison.

Only the exact original 107 evaluation dates and ten warmup dates are authorized
for the fixed 32-profile comparison. This historical test is not broker
operational validation, account sizing, payout estimation or live approval.
