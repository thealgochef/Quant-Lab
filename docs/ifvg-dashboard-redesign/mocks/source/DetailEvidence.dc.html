What this result can't tell you

· One historical path, January 13 – June 10, 2026. It compares configurations on that path; it isn't a probability of future payouts.
· A replacement account is assumed available instantly. No evaluation or purchase delay is modeled.
· Entries fill at the confirming candle's closing trade. Stops fill at the first recorded trade at or through the stop — not broker execution, and no queue position or liquidity.
· An entry refused during a payout pause throws that setup away. The strategy may form new setups afterward.
· The period ends at the last day's 4:00 PM close. A payout paid exactly then counts; anything later shows as pending.
· Where a minute's recorded trades don't rebuild the candle exactly, that minute uses a labeled approximation, losing side first.
· Firm terms are your simulation terms, not a claim of full compliance with each live program.
· Strategy measures use the engine's candle rules; funded results use the recorded-trade order. The two can differ within a minute.
