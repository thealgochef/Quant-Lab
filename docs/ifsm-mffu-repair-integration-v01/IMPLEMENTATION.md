## Reporting definitions

`propsim/funded/reporting_accounts.py` resolves `account_number` and legacy
`account_id` at the reporting boundary. Conflicting fields fail attribution.
The canonical key includes economic result, configuration, firm and account.
Read adapters derive copies; original balances, fees and event bytes are untouched.
Saved-result analysis v2 uses the same resolver. Historical v1 remains readable
and is verified under its original account projection.

`propsim/funded/reporting_legs.py` reconciles actual quantities, prices and posted
costs. Per-fill cost remains $0.514 times actual micros, rounded half-up once
after multiplication. Saved individual fees take precedence when present and
must reconcile to the frozen rule and total costs. Entry fee allocation floors
the first leg's proportional integer-cent share; the final leg gets the residual.
Allocated costs sum to the posted entry cost, and leg nets sum to trade net.

Whole-position deadline exits and actual partial exits with a remainder at the
deadline are separate populations. In the original MCB062 result, 32 genuine
remaining halves contributed $67,345.52 net; six whole deadline positions
contributed -$256.68. Every one of the original 12,556 funded trades reconciles;
all 425 account concentration rows match the supplied independent supplement.

## Normal dashboard and reporting views

The usual launcher is `streamlit run scripts/dashboard.py`. Open **My studies**,
then **IFSM MFFU context batch**. The catalog stores verified external-result
references additively; the result-version picker preserves the original version.
Reading a result uses an immutable compact companion and does not install the
task Core. The registered-input Market conditions provider reads the exact
263-date Task B one-minute source, including the ten warmup dates, and never
falls back to another study. Retrospective price labels use that day's completed
session; known-at-entry labels use only previously completed sessions.

**Gamma & expected move** defaults to actual funded entries over the full saved
operation. Ordinary candle replay remains a separately identified population.
Entry and first-target checkpoints are distinct, with quantities and actual
funded subsecond event times retained. Negative, early-positive and established-
positive groups are disjoint; their counts sum to the selected eligible
population. Context age means eligible report age under the saved nominal
10:00 PM Chicago availability rule, rather than measured publication latency.
Carried, missing, posthoc and left-censored evidence stays explicit. Geometry
groups use unique parent locks, actual frozen tick distances and fallback status.
The original 302 MCB062 funded locks span 17–212 ticks, with a 117-tick median.

Trade review has separately labeled full-history and point-in-time modes. At
a point-in-time cursor, future accounts, fills, totals and outcomes remain
hidden. Historical eligible EOD map segments can appear only from their own
availability boundary; later maps cannot extend backward. A first-target
checkpoint becomes visible at its saved event time, including subsecond timing.

**Compare by** offers total net cash, payout speed, building the flat cushion,
lower account usage, regular activity and larger payouts. Each uses the exact
documented tuple order with stable configuration-ID ties and unavailable values
last. No weighted score is introduced. **Fit my targets** is disabled by default;
enabled constraints combine with AND. The optional one-trade-per-date preset is
0.8–1.3 average entries over all 253 evaluated dates, including zero-entry dates.
Empty results remain empty unless the user opts into closest matches, which
display each violated requirement and the number of failed requirements.
Two to four selected alternatives can be compared side by side. Exports retain
the selected version, population, filters, targets, sort and metric definitions.

Operation clocks start at the original operation opening. Account clocks start
at each actual purchase and retain failed, ongoing and unavailable accounts.
Flat balance at +$2,000, secured eligibility, request and received payment are
separate milestones. Conditional medians include only accounts that reached
the milestone; all-account status counts remain visible alongside them.

Shared reporting schema `ifsm_mffu_reporting_v5` supplies both the UI and review
tables. Its source identity is separate from the economic plan. UI preferences
are saved independently of immutable studies and version-aware caches avoid
rescanning full decision traces on each filter change.
Revision v5 changes only a wrapped reporting-source line and the revision
identifier; its computed values are checked against preserved v4. Earlier
reporting publications remain immutable historical evidence.

## Scope and limits

The saved 253 evaluated dates and ten warmup dates are unchanged. Views compare
alternative successive-account operations; their cash is never added together.
Received payouts minus all actual account acquisitions remains the economic
objective. Pending receipts are separate. Reporting filters do not change an
entry policy or launch a worker. No new study hypotheses, date expansion,
protected prices, live promotion or production pin change are authorized.

Vendor EOD levels use `NQ1!` continuous-front-month coordinates without an
independently established futures-basis adjustment. Nominal availability is an
executed study assumption, not proof of the vendor's publication time. Ordinary
historical controls retain posthoc context annotations and their original
execution source. Reporting associations do not establish a forward strategy
edge, payout attribution or a new research hypothesis.
